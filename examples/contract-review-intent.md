# Intent: Demo Legal Contract Review and Redlining AI

## Project Configuration

- **Project Name:** demo-contract-review
- **App Type:** api
- **Data Stores:** blob, cosmos
- **Region:** eastus2
- **Environment:** dev
- **Auth:** entra-id
- **Compliance:** SOC2

## Problem Statement

Legal team manually reviews 200+ vendor contracts per year, spending 4-8 hours
each. Missed clauses have caused 3 incidents in 18 months. Cost: $120K+ in
legal staff time plus liability exposure.

## Business Goals

- Reduce contract review time by 75% (from 4-8 hours to under 60 minutes)
- Achieve 100% coverage of required protective clauses
- Reduce legal staff time cost by $90K+ annually
- Enable self-service redlining for procurement officers

## Target Users

1. **Legal Staff** -- Reviews contracts, negotiates clauses, manages risk
2. **Procurement Officer** -- Uploads contracts, views analysis, forwards to legal
3. **Department Head** -- Read-only access to contract risk summaries
