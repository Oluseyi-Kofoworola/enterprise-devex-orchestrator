# global-supply-chain-platform

> AI-powered global supply chain resilience platform for multi-modal logistics
> with disruption prediction, dynamic rerouting, and supplier risk scoring.

## Configuration

- App Type: api
- Data Stores: cosmos, sql, redis
- Region: eastus2
- Environment: dev
- Auth: managed-identity
- Compliance: SOC2

## Problem Statement

Global manufacturer with $4.2B annual procurement across 340 suppliers in 28
countries loses $180M/year to supply chain disruptions. Average disruption
detection takes 72 hours, rerouting takes 5 days, and 23% of shipments miss
delivery windows due to lack of real-time visibility.

## Business Goals

- Reduce disruption detection time from 72 hours to under 4 hours
- Cut rerouting decision time from 5 days to 8 hours with AI recommendations
- Improve on-time delivery from 77% to 94%
- Reduce safety stock costs by 30% via demand-sensing analytics

## Target Users

1. **Supply Chain Director** -- Monitors global risk dashboard, approves rerouting
2. **Procurement Manager** -- Evaluates supplier risk scores, manages purchase orders
3. **Logistics Coordinator** -- Tracks shipments, manages port and carrier schedules
4. **Risk Analyst** -- Reviews disruption predictions, runs scenario simulations
